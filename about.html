Who we are

Built around care, quality and trust.

GRAND VIEW CERAMIC AND MARBLE WORK is based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Our brand is built around a simple idea: the small details make a meaningful difference to how a space looks and works.

We aim to begin each project with a clear understanding of the client's expectations, site conditions and practical constraints. From there, careful preparation and good communication help guide the work through to completion.

We welcome enquiries for tile and marble work, along with plumbing, pipe fitting and electrical service requirements. The exact scope is confirmed case by case.
